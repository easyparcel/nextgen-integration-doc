# EasyParcel Wix Integration

---

## Introduction
This guide will walk you through integrating NextGen EasyParcel with Wix. With this integration, you'll import orders from Wix into your EasyParcel account for fulfillment.

---

### Setup Integrated App
**Step 1:** Under **EasyConnect**, click on **Installed Ecommerce Apps**, find **Wix** you may find your Wix app successfully install.
<img width="1402" height="521" alt="Screenshot 2026-01-26 at 9 48 22 PM" src="https://github.com/user-attachments/assets/c5bf14e8-03dd-4f49-b363-a8428a414d6d" />


**Step 2:** In the store, click **Sender Address** and key in your sender details and click on the **Update** button.
<img width="1387" height="461" alt="Screenshot 2026-01-26 at 9 52 18 PM" src="https://github.com/user-attachments/assets/7980f34e-6fa1-4073-a599-fc823753a84e" />


**Done ! You have successfully setup Wix app in easyparcel.**
