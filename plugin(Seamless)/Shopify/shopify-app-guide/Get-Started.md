## 🚪 1. Getting In: Opening the App
 
Finding the app is easier than finding parking in a mall on a weekend:
 
1. Log in to your **Shopify admin**.
2. In the left sidebar, scroll down to **Apps**.
3. Click **EasyParcel- Delivery Made Easy**.
Boom. You're in. 🎉
 
> 💡 **First time here?** Before shipping anything, head to **Shopify → Settings → Checkout** and set **Shipping address phone number** to **Required**. Couriers need your customer's phone number to deliver, and this makes sure the app always gets it.

## 🧠 3. Pro Tips & Common Oopsies
 
### ⭐ Pro tips
 
- **Check the Dashboard every day.** It takes 5 seconds and tells you if anyone's waiting.
- **Turn on Auto Fulfillment** once you're comfortable. Future you will say thanks.
- **Use bulk fulfilment for sale days.** 11.11 and 12.12 are not the time for one-by-one shipping. 🛍️
- **Use the search bar** to jump straight to a specific order.
### 😅 Common oopsies (and quick fixes)
 
| Uh-oh… | Try this |
|---|---|
| "My new order isn't showing up!" | Click **Sync Orders from Shopify**. Also check it's marked **Paid**. |
| "The courier can't reach my customer." | Make phone numbers **Required** at Shopify checkout (see Section 1). |
| "Auto Fulfillment didn't ship my order." | Check your **EasyParcel credit**, then look at the **Auto Fulfillment log**. |
| "The pickup went to my old address." | Update your **sender details** in **Setting**. |
| "My Dashboard shows nothing." | New setup? Sync and ship your first order — the numbers will appear. |
